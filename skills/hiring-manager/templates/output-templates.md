# Hiring-manager output templates

One template per mode. All outputs save to `outputs/hiring/[role]/`; filenames noted per template. Drop sections that don't apply; never leave them empty.

## Mode 1: Job description (`jd-[role-name]-[date].md`)

```markdown
# [Role Title], [Department]

**Location:** [City, State (work model)]
**Compensation:** [Range] + benefits
**Reports to:** [Title]

## About This Role
[2-3 sentences. What problem does this person solve? Why does this role exist now?]

## What You'll Do
[5-7 responsibilities as action-oriented bullets. Specific outcomes, not vague duties.]

## Day in the Life
[One realistic workday as a short paragraph. Makes the role tangible.]

## What Success Looks Like (First 90 Days)
[3-5 specific, measurable outcomes]

## What You'll Learn
[3-5 honest growth opportunities]

## Qualifications

**Required**
[3-5 items, job-related only, never proxies for protected classes]

**Preferred**
[3-5 items]

## How to Apply
[Submission instructions. Consider a signal question to test engagement and screen bots.]
```

## Mode 2: Screening report (`[candidate]-screen-[date].md`)

```markdown
# Candidate Screening: [Candidate Name]
**Role:** [Role Title] · **Date:** [Date]
**Overall Recommendation:** Advance / Hold / Pass

## Submission Completeness
- [ ] Resume · [ ] Cover letter / statement · [ ] Work samples
- [ ] Signal question: Pass / Fail / Not applicable

## Qualification Scorecard

| Requirement | Evidence in submission | Rating |
|---|---|---|
| [Req from JD] | [Quote or paraphrase from resume] | Strong / Partial / Gap |

**Required met:** [X of Y] · **Preferred met:** [X of Y]

## Resume Claims to Verify
- "[Exact quote]", needs: [what evidence or question would confirm it]

## Gaps and Anomalies
[Employment gaps, short tenures, vague timeframes; note without speculation, mark for interview probing]

## Strengths
[2-4, each tied to a role requirement]

## Concerns
[2-4, job-related only]

## Suggested Next Step
[Advance / Hold pending X / Pass, with brief rationale]
```

## Mode 3: Research report (`[candidate]-research-[date].md`)

```markdown
# Candidate Research: [Candidate Name]
**Role:** [Role Title] · **Date:** [Date]
**Searches Conducted:** [list]

## Professional Presence
[LinkedIn/portfolio/published work: quality, consistency with resume]

## Findings Relevant to Role Requirements

| Finding | Source | Reliability | Relevance |
|---|---|---|---|
| | [URL/platform] | Confirmed / Unconfirmed | [tie to a requirement] |

## Discrepancies from Resume
[Public information contradicting stated claims, with sources]

## Concerns
[Job-relevant only, each tied to a requirement]

## Nothing Concerning Found
[Use this section when research was clean]

## EEOC Note
"Research was conducted for job-relevant professional conduct only. Protected-class
information encountered during research was set aside and is not reflected here."
```

## Mode 4: Interview prep (`[candidate]-interview-prep-[date].md`)

```markdown
# Interview Prep: [Candidate Name]
**Role:** [Role Title] · **Date:** [Date] · **Format:** [Phone / Video / In-person]

## Interview Approach
[2-3 sentences: what to focus on for this specific candidate]

## Section 1: Verify Key Claims
**Q: [Question]**
*Testing:* [resume claim being validated]
*Strong answer:* [indicators of genuine depth]
*Weak answer:* [vague generalities, no specifics]

## Section 2: Probe Gaps and Anomalies
[Same Q format, targeting items from the screen report]

## Section 3: Role-Specific Scenarios
[2-3 scenarios drawn from actual JD responsibilities, same Q format]

## Section 4: Culture and Working Style
[1-2 questions tied to how this team actually works]

## Illegal Questions to Avoid
[Candidate-specific pitfalls, e.g. cover letter mentioned family: follow-ups are off-limits]

## Logistics
- [ ] Confirm role details, location, compensation range
- [ ] Explain process and timeline
- [ ] "Do you have questions about the role or the team?"
```

## Mode 5: Interview debrief (`[candidate]-debrief-[date].md`)

```markdown
# Interview Debrief: [Candidate Name]
**Role:** [Role Title] · **Interview date:** [Date] · **Interviewer(s):** [Names]
**Overall Signal:** Strong Advance / Advance / Neutral / Lean Pass / Pass

## Key Moments, Strengths
**[Topic]**: "[quote or paraphrase]", why it matters for this role

## Key Moments, Concerns
**[Topic]**: "[quote or paraphrase]", what it signals, job-relevant only

## Follow-Up Items
- [Item]: why it matters, how to resolve (reference check, follow-up question, work sample)

## Claims Made in Interview to Verify
[New verbal claims not on the resume; verify before offer]

## Red Flags
[Job-relevant only: dishonesty signals, concerning treatment of colleagues, core-competency gaps]

## EEOC Note
[If protected-class information surfaced: "Candidate mentioned [category]. Noted and
set aside per EEOC guidelines; not factored into this evaluation."]

## Recommended Next Step
[Advance / references / final decision / pass, with rationale]
```
