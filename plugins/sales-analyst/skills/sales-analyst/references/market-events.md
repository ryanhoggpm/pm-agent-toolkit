## Macro events (apply to most enterprise IT / IoT hardware)

| Period | Event | Impact direction | Most affected |
|---|---|---|---|

| 2020 | COVID-19 onset | Positive: remote access, OOB, branch connectivity demand spike | OOB, serial console, remote access, VPN appliances |

| 2023-present | AI infrastructure buildout | Positive: data center expansion, edge compute growth | DC OOB, high-density console servers |
